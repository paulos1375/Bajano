The villa is spacious, tastefully decorated and equipped with every comfort. Everything is excellently maintained, from the comfortable bedrooms and modern bathrooms to the fully equipped kitchen. The beautiful garden and the pool are also kept in perfect condition.

We just returned from a visit to Barbara's villa, which was perfect for our stay. We were two couples and there was plenty of room. The kitchen had all we needed for cooking meals, we had breakfast every day outside on the patio and most dinners there too. The BBQ was clean and worked great. We preferred the less crowded beaches further away but did get to Jan Thiel once and saw a magnificent sunset there. Close by via car is Tugboat Beach with cool snorkeling and a couple restaurants. We ate at Brisa del Mar and that was delicious! PINK was there to meet us and show us the villa and the communication all around was great.
